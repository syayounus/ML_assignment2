Deployment checklist:

5. Use app.py as the main file.
6. Deploy and copy the Streamlit app link.
7. Replace placeholder links in README.md and ML_Assignment_2_Submission_Draft.pdf before final submission.
8. Run on BITS Virtual Lab and add the screenshot to your final PDF.
